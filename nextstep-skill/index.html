Small decision-support skill

What should I do right now?

NextStep turns a crowded list of worries and tasks into one clear next action—without giving you another giant productivity plan.

“You do not need to solve everything. You only need to decide the next action.”

It prioritizes

Urgency, importance, controllability, effort, dependencies and what unlocks other work.

It parks

Waiting, future uncertainty, and things that matter but cannot be acted on right now.

Do now: Prepare the 5-minute introduction for the meeting that happens first.

Ignore for now: Whether someone replies today.

Then: Move to the next controllable deadline.
