# Example — PhD application overwhelm

## Input

I have a supervisor meeting next Tuesday, a paper revision, three emails waiting for replies, and I also need to look for jobs. I feel like I should be doing all of them.

## Output

**Do now:** Draft the 5-minute introduction for Tuesday's supervisor meeting.

**Why:** It is the nearest high-impact deadline and you can make progress on it immediately.

**Ignore for now:** The three unanswered emails. You cannot control whether they reply today.

**Then:** Spend one focused block on the paper revision that has the closest deadline.
